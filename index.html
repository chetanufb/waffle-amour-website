Our story

A Belgian classic,
made with love.

In Belgium, the waffle is more than a snack. It's the warm smell drifting from a street corner, a treat shared after school, a little ritual at the end of a long day.

Waffle Amour brings that feeling home. Our Liège waffles are made in Belgium from a rich, buttery dough studded with pearl sugar that caramelises in the iron, giving every bite its signature crunch. Some we dip in smooth chocolate; others we leave beautifully simple.

Alongside them sits our Belgian style range: light, golden toasting waffles and dusted heart waffles, made to be dressed up however you like.

Serving suggestions

Make it yours

Straight from the pack, warmed or toasted, there's no wrong way to enjoy a Waffle Amour.

♥
01

Warm & melty

Pop a Liège waffle in a warm oven for a few minutes: the sugar pearls soften and the chocolate turns glossy.

02

Toast & top

Toast a Belgian style waffle until crisp, then pile on strawberries, ice cream and a drizzle of chocolate.

03

Dessert in minutes

Layer heart or hazelnut waffles with whipped cream and berries for an easy, show-stopping pudding.
